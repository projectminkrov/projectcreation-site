About ProjectCreation

ProjectCreation is an AI-powered developer tools suite at projectcreation.net. It combines three purpose-built systems — ProjectCipher, ProjectWord, and ProjectBuilt — under one subscription.

ProjectCipher is a macOS app that runs up to 12 AI agents simultaneously in a single terminal room. Each agent operates in its own pane — no tab-switching, no lost threads, no manual stitching between tools.

ProjectWord is a native macOS dictation engine powered by Whisper AI. It runs entirely offline and types your spoken words wherever your cursor is — in any app, any field, at full speed.

ProjectBuilt is an AI planning partner that transforms a rough idea into a concrete, researched, step-by-step project plan so you can start building immediately.

All three tools are covered by a single ProjectCreation subscription starting at €8/month, with a 3-day Pro trial and no card required to start.
