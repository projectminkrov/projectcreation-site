// 04 — Questions

Before you start the trial.

What happens after the 3-day Pro trial?

You get full Pro-level access for 3 days with no card required. When the trial ends, your account moves to whichever tier you choose — there's no surprise charge, because nothing's on file until you decide to subscribe.

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes. Core, Pro, and Max are steps on the same ladder, not separate products — move up when you need more terminals, automation, or workspace headroom, or back down when you don't.

What's the difference between monthly and yearly billing?

Yearly billing bundles 12 months for the price of 9 — three months free compared to paying monthly. Same features, same tier, just less of it going to billing overhead.

Do I need all three tools to get value from one account?

No. Every tier includes ProjectWord and ProjectBuilt plus a slice of ProjectCipher — use whichever tools fit your workflow. The tier just controls how much room you have in each.
